# NNNN. Decision stated as a short phrase

* Status: Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
* Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play: what is true, what is constrained, what made a decision necessary.
Facts, not arguments.

## Decision

What is decided, in full sentences and the active voice.

## Alternatives considered

Each reasonable alternative and why it was turned down. This is the part that stops the
same discussion from starting again.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision, good and bad, including what becomes harder.
